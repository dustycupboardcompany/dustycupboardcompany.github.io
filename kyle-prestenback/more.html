A closer look at the work behind the work.

BUILT
IN THE FIELD.

I build experience systems where story, technology, service, and operations meet—and stay close enough to the work to make sure they survive real conditions.

That has meant working aboard active cruise ships, inside global entertainment portfolios, alongside hospitality teams, and across the commercial realities of growing businesses.

Hospitality systems 01

Designed with the people who run the room.

At Carnival Corporation, I led the development and deployment of restaurant and bar technology within the MedallionClass experience platform. The work included yield optimization, order and kitchen routing, POS-connected guest interfaces, and the operational systems behind thousands of food-and-beverage service points.

The most useful design work happened in service: embedded aboard ships, working directly with bar teams, restaurant leaders, and back-of-house staff to understand pacing, sectioning, ticket flow, and the realities of high-volume hospitality.

  • 01Observe the real rhythm

    Work shoulder-to-shoulder with operators during live service, not only in workshops.

  • 02Translate operations into systems

    Turn service timing, staffing patterns, and kitchen realities into tools people can actually use.

  • 03Design for people

    Build technology that supports judgment and hospitality instead of adding another dashboard.

Story-driven service 02

Commercial execution 03

Creative ambition needs a business model.

At Hunt A Killer, I expanded beyond experience development into product, growth, marketing, sales, partnerships, and small-business operations during a volatile period for the company.

The role sharpened how I connect narrative depth to customer journeys, campaign performance, inventory pacing, channel strategy, and repeatable revenue.

Operating principle

An experience becomes durable when creative quality, commercial performance, and cross-functional delivery reinforce one another.

Interactive worlds 04

Systems that make a world respond.

My work across Walt Disney Imagineering and Carnival helped translate emerging technologies into real guest experiences—from persistent identity and responsive environments to personalization, mobile tools, passive inputs, and media-triggered interactions.

Projects including Star Wars: Galaxy’s Edge, MyMagic+, and MedallionClass demanded more than a compelling prototype. They required scalable logic, operational resilience, and technology subtle enough to strengthen the human experience rather than compete with it.

Studio practice 05

From possibility to an operating plan.

Through Dusty Cupboard Company, I bring this operating perspective to entertainment, hospitality, and brand experiences—helping teams shape the guest promise, interactive architecture, technical approach, delivery roadmap, and investment case.

The through-line is consistent: multi-path experiences that respond to behavior, invisible technology that supports human interaction, and a clear balance between narrative, throughput, maintainability, and performance.
