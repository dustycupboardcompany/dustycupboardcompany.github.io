What happened next 30—Now

The dreams arrived.
The building continued.

The years that followed made the canvas larger: whole fleets, operating systems, new ventures, opening-day realities, and a family at the center of it all.

2011—2016

Imagineering at scale

Portfolio leadership for interactive and immersive attractions, including Test Track, MagicBand, personalized park experiences, Star Wars, Innoventions, and Marvel development.

2016—2022

From prototype to a fleet

At Carnival Corporation, I helped stabilize and scale the approximately $1B Princess MedallionClass transformation across ships, services, physical environments, and guest operations.

2021—Now

Building the company, too

Dusty Cupboard Company, Hunt A Killer, Wynn Resorts, Warner Bros., Secret Cinema, cultural organizations, and Next Byte expanded the work into ventures, commercial strategy, financing, construction, and launch.

The real center

Heather, the kids, and the pets

Home is life with Heather, our kids, and a cast of pets who make every schedule more interesting—and every accomplishment more meaningful.

Still becoming ∞

The story is still under construction.

The work keeps changing. The through-line does not: stay curious, make the difficult thing clearer, help the team move, and build something that holds up when people finally step inside it.
